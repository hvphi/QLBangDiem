# Internal verification contract v1

Signature count depends on the workflow stage: lecturer upload requires at least one verified embedded signature and creates `LECTURER_SIGNED`, visible in the department-head queue with a `SUBMISSION_RECEIVED` notification. Department approval separately requires at least two verified signatures before archiving. The shared verifier must evaluate a one-signature PDF rather than reject it merely for missing the department-head signature.

Core sends one PDF as multipart `file` to `POST /internal/v1/verify` with `X-Service-Token`. The isolated service returns the SHA-256, overall status (`VALID`, `INVALID`, `REVOKED`, `EXPIRED`, `UNKNOWN`, or `UNAVAILABLE`), signature count, signer metadata, and stable reason codes. Requests are limited to 20 MiB. The endpoint must remain inside the core-to-verification network boundary. A PDF without a signature or with a broken signed byte range is `INVALID`. The current bridge uses pyHanko's `intact` result and returns `VALID` with `SIGNATURES_INTACT_TRUST_NOT_CHECKED`; this checks signed-content integrity, not certificate trust, signer identity, revocation, or the CMS cryptographic `valid` result. Full PKI validation remains a separate production requirement.
